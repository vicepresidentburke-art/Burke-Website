A Typical Evening

What to Expect

1. Opening

The meeting is called to order, minutes and announcements are read, and the evening's resolution or program is introduced.

2. Presentations & Debate

Members deliver short literary presentations, followed by a formal debate between two teams on the evening's resolution.

3. Discussion

The floor opens to general discussion and questions before the meeting adjourns.
